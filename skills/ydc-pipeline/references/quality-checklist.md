# YDC Pipeline: Quality Checklist

Before delivering outputs for any account, verify:

- [ ] Account plan covers all 9 sections with no sections skipped (including Outreach Strategy appendix)
- [ ] All externally sourced claims have real citations (not fabricated URLs)
- [ ] AE-only fields are left blank, not filled with assumptions
- [ ] You.com value narrative is woven into Solution Mapping (Section 5) with specific use cases
- [ ] All 4 outreach sequences are present and personalized to the account
- [ ] Zero em dashes in any outreach copy
- [ ] Founder credibility appears in at least one touch per sequence
- [ ] Third-party validation included in at least one sequence (anonymized evals, case studies, or benchmarks; NEVER name confidential customers)
- [ ] Subject lines are specific (reference company name or initiative)
- [ ] Email word counts within guidelines (opener 100-150, follow-up 80-120, breakup 80-120)
- [ ] LinkedIn connect notes use Recognition + Curiosity Hook + Connect formula; under 250 chars; zero pitch, zero CTA, zero flattery, zero role claims; problem seed planted without naming product
- [ ] No generic/templated language that could apply to any company
- [ ] Every email follows the AIDA micro-structure (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action)
- [ ] No banned anti-pattern phrases appear in any outreach copy
- [ ] Every opener starts with THEM (their news, their initiative, their challenge), not with "I," "We," "Our," or "At You.com"
- [ ] Zero markdown formatting in email bodies (no bold asterisks, no headers, no numbered lists)
- [ ] Each email contains exactly one proof point and one CTA, never more
- [ ] Hook prioritization followed: trigger events used when available, not defaulting to role-based pain
- [ ] Paragraphs in email copy are 2-3 sentences max
- [ ] Interest-based CTAs only in cold outreach (no time-based asks like "15 minutes" or "Tuesday at 2pm")
- [ ] Tentative hypothesis language used in Interest section (not asserting pain as fact)
- [ ] Zero AI-ism vocabulary (utilize, comprehensive, enhance, delve, embark, robust, streamline)
- [ ] No AI glazing: every research hook bridges to a problem or opportunity we solve. Never a compliment that dead-ends ("strong signal," "that's rare," "impressive achievement"). State what they did, tie it to relevance.
- [ ] 5th-7th grade reading level (short sentences, no compound-complex structures)
- [ ] No corporate suffixes (write "Teradata" not "Teradata Corporation")
- [ ] Account plan uploaded to Google Drive under "Account Plans, Lists & Personalized Sequences/{Company}/"
- [ ] Google Drive folder structure verified with rclone tree
- [ ] 4 Apollo sequences created (INACTIVE) with correct naming convention (YDC | {Company} | Seq {A-D})
- [ ] All sequence steps match personalized copy from Step 4 (correct subjects, bodies, step types)
- [ ] All contacts with verified emails imported to Apollo (dedup enabled)
- [ ] Company account created in Apollo (or existing account reused)
- [ ] Contacts labeled with "Whale Pipeline" and "{Company} - Seq {A|B|C|D}"
- [ ] Contacts enrolled in correct sequences
- [ ] Apollo import summary displayed with INACTIVE status confirmed
- [ ] No-email contacts logged for manual LinkedIn outreach

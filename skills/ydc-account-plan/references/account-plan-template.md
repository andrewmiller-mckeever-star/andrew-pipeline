# Account Plan Template Structure

```
# ACCOUNT PLAN: {Company Name}

## 1. Account Overview
- Company Name
- Website
- HQ Location
- Employee Count (with citation)
- Annual Revenue (with citation)
- Industry
- Business Units
- Public Tech Stack (AI, Data, Infra) (with citations)
- Recent Press Releases (with citations)
- Public Initiatives Related to AI or Digital Transformation (with citations)
- Existing Relationship Notes: [AE-ONLY, leave blank]
- Renewal Details: [AE-ONLY, leave blank]
- Internal Ownership: [AE-ONLY, leave blank]

## 2. Strategic Business Context
- Corporate Strategy Summary (with citations)
- Industry Trends Impacting the Company (with citations)
- Public AI / Automation Programs (with citations)
- Earnings Call Themes (with citations if public company)
- Market Pressures and Competitive Forces (with citations)
- Hypothesized Priorities (To Be Validated by AE)
- Hypothesized Metrics (To Be Validated by AE)

## 3. Buying Center & Relationship Map
- Stakeholder Directory: table with Name, Title, Relevance to You.com, LinkedIn URL
- Potential Influencers (Inferred)
- Possible Champion Candidates (To Be Validated by AE)
- Economic Buyer: [AE-ONLY, leave blank]
- Champion: [AE-ONLY, leave blank]
- Additional Key Stakeholders: [AE-ONLY, leave blank]

## 4. Customer Pain & Business Impact
- Public or Industry Pain Points Likely Relevant (with citations)
- Signals Indicating Data, Search, or AI Challenges
- Hypothesized Customer Pains (To Be Validated by AE)
- Hypothesized Business Impact (To Be Validated by AE)
- Confirmed Customer Pains: [AE-ONLY, leave blank]
- Quantified Impact: [AE-ONLY, leave blank]
- Current Internal Workarounds: [AE-ONLY, leave blank]

## 5. Solution Mapping & Use Case Design
- Recommended Use Cases leveraging You.com Search APIs (To Be Validated by AE)
- Recommended Agentic Workflows powered by You.com (To Be Validated by AE)
- Opportunities to leverage AI Factory-As-A-Service (To Be Validated by AE)
- Hypothesized Success Metrics
- Final Prioritized Use Cases: [AE-ONLY, leave blank]
- Confirmed Technical Requirements: [AE-ONLY, leave blank]
- Integration Points: [AE-ONLY, leave blank]

## 6. Competitive / Risk Analysis
- Known Competitors (to You.com, for this specific deal)
- Public Product or Capability Comparisons
- Potential Deal Risks (To Be Validated by AE)
- Actual Competitive Position: [AE-ONLY, leave blank]
- Internally Communicated Risks: [AE-ONLY, leave blank]

## 7. ROI & Value Justification
- Baseline Assumptions (To Be Validated by AE)
- Hypothesized ROI tied to You.com capabilities
- Validated ROI Inputs: [AE-ONLY, leave blank]
- Final Value Narrative for the Economic Buyer: [AE-ONLY, leave blank]

## 8. Outreach Strategy (Appendix)
- Sequence Overview: table with Sequence (A/B/C/D), Persona Target, Use Case Focus, Primary Hook Type
- Contact Assignments: table with Name, Title, Email, Sequence, Assignment Rationale
- Hook Strategy: 2-3 sentences per sequence on which research hooks to prioritize (trigger events, content, initiatives)
- Founder Credibility Placement: which sequence/touch gets which proof point (avoid repetition across contacts at same company)
- Notes: any account-specific sequencing decisions (e.g., "Skip Seq D, no AI/ML leaders identified")
```

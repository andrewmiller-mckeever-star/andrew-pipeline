The eval

The Research API trial ran on Northwind's own test set from Aug 26 to Sep 22. Jordan Blake ran it with Priya.
75%
Confidence we win
My estimate. The champion and the budget are real. The risk is the clock and a CFO we have not met.

Where we win

  • Their own test set. 87% correct on 200 real analyst questions, with a source for each answer.
  • Time saved. Draft time fell from 6 hours to about 2.5 hours per report in the trial.
  • Already a customer. Security reviewed us in March. Much of the questionnaire repeats.
  • One bill. Elena likes having search and research on one contract.

Where we fall short

  • Long questions. Accuracy drops to 78% on questions over 40 words.
  • Quarry Search is cheaper. Their quote was about 15% lower. Elena says quality matters more, for now.
  • No CFO contact. Grace Kim has never heard our pitch. She signs over $100K.
  • Tight clock. Four weeks of paper in a six-week window.
Deal thesis

Northwind already trusts us with their pricing engine. The Research API saves each analyst about 3.5 hours per report. With 60 analysts, that is the case Elena will take to the CFO. We win if we finish security fast, meet Grace before signature, and close the accuracy gap on long questions.

  1. Send the questionnaire by 10/13. Leo finishes the last 23 answers. Dana checks the retention answers.
  2. Meet Grace Kim. Ask Elena for a short intro. Bring the time-saved math, not a demo.
  3. Close the accuracy gap. Priya ships the long-question prompt fix and reruns the test set before go-live.
  4. Line up the next unit. Plan the Contents API eval with Jordan so it starts the week after signature.
